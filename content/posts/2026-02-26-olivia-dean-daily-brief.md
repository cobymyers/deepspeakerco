## Start here: Man I Need
[Listen to The Art of Loving on Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/the-art-of-loving/1817609404), beginning with **“Man I Need.”** It is one of the singles from Olivia Dean’s second album, released in September 2025. As an entry point, it gives you a specific song to spend time with before taking on the whole record.

## Three songs, three ways in
1. **Man I Need.** Start with the direct statement at the heart of the title. Then move through the album to hear how that certainty sits alongside other versions of love.
2. **So Easy (To Fall in Love).** Dean’s official album introduction describes its joyful rhythms and hopeful writing. Pick this next if you want the record’s lighter side.
3. **Nice to Each Other.** Dean has described this song as an exploration of independence in dating. Listen for the tension between wanting closeness and holding onto your own space.

## Make room for the whole record
Produced with Zach Nahome, **The Art of Loving** considers romantic love alongside friendship and self-reflection. That range is the reason to continue beyond the singles: the album gives the individual songs a broader conversation to belong to.

Try the three songs above, then restart the album from the beginning. Treat that second pass as a chance to notice how the running order changes your first impression.
