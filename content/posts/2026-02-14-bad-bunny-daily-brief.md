## Start with the dance
[Watch the official “BAILE INoLVIDABLE” video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a1Femq4NPxs). The song’s salsa arrangement offers a specific starting point for **DeBÍ TiRAR MáS FOToS**, Bad Bunny’s 2025 album.

## Listen for the ensemble
Apple Music’s album notes connect “BAILE INoLVIDABLE” to the salsa tradition of Willie Colón and Héctor Lavoe. Let that be an invitation to listen beyond the lead vocal: follow the instrumental exchanges and give the closing section time to unfold.

## Keep the album close
Next, play **“DtMF”** and **“NUEVAYoL”** from [the album](https://music.apple.com/us/album/1787022393). Rather than treating those tracks as interchangeable hits, compare how each builds its own setting. Which one makes you want to hear the next song, and which sends you back to the beginning?

The album’s Puerto Rican focus provides the wider context. Once you have a favourite, listen in sequence to hear where that track sits in the record as a whole.
